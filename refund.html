Refund policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

If The Boxing Folder isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of buying it and you'll get your money back in full. You don't need to give a reason.

How to ask

  • Write to us through the contact page within 14 days of your purchase.
  • Use the email address you bought with, or include it in your message, so we can find your order.
  • A reason is welcome but never required.

What happens next

We reply within 2 business days and issue the refund for the full amount you paid, including any tax charged. You'll get it back on the payment method you used: dollars if you paid in dollars, rupees if you paid in rupees.

Payments and refunds are processed by Polar, our online reseller and merchant of record, so the refund shows up from Polar. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it on your statement.

Once you've been refunded, your download access ends. Please delete any copies of the PDF you saved.

After 14 days

We don't normally refund purchases after 14 days. If something has gone wrong, for example you were charged twice or the file won't open, write to us anyway and we'll put it right.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under the consumer law where you live.
