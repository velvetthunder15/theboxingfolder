Refund policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Asking for a refund

If you'd like a refund, email us at samkennedy324@gmail.com. You can also use the contact page.

  • Send it from the email address you bought with, or include that address in your message, so we can find your order.
  • Tell us briefly what went wrong. It helps us improve the folder.

We reply within 2 business days and let you know what we can do.

Failed payments

If your payment fails or is declined, you aren't charged and nothing needs to be refunded. If your bank put a temporary hold on the amount, it releases the hold automatically, usually within a few business days.

Charged by mistake

If you were charged twice, or charged but the order didn't go through, the extra or failed charge is refunded automatically. If you don't see it back within 10 business days, email us and we'll sort it out straight away.

Download problems

If the PDF won't download or open, email us first. We'll send you a working copy. If we can't fix it, you get a full refund.

How refunds are paid

Payments and refunds are handled by Polar, our online reseller and merchant of record, so a refund shows up from Polar. It goes back to the payment method you used, in the currency you paid: dollars if you paid in dollars, rupees if you paid in rupees. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it on your statement.

Once you've been refunded, your download access ends. Please delete any copies of the PDF you saved.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under the consumer law where you live.
