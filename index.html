FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need any equipment?

No. Wear comfortable clothes, and either shoes you can pivot in or bare feet on a floor that doesn't slip. A mirror helps but isn't required.

How much space do I need?

About 2 × 2 metres. Clear anything you could hit or trip on, and check for ceiling fans before you throw an uppercut.

I have an injury or a health condition. Can I do this?

Talk to your doctor first. Shadowboxing is low impact, but it still raises your heart rate and works your shoulders, knees and back. Stop if anything hurts beyond normal effort.

How is it delivered?

How long is each session?

20 to 30 minutes. Short enough to fit before work or after dinner.

Safety note

Train safe

If you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, are pregnant, or are recovering from an injury, check with a doctor before you start. Warm up, stay in control, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
