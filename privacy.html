Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy explains what The Boxing Folder ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, and what we do with it. It's short because we collect very little.

The short version

  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or trackers.
  • Fonts and images come from the site itself, so your browser doesn't contact other companies to show you the page.
  • We only receive personal information if you write to us or buy the folder.

When you browse the website

Our hosting provider, Render, delivers the pages. Like any host, it may record basic technical details for each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the time, to keep the service running and secure. We don't use this to identify or profile you.

To show prices in your currency, the page asks our host which country your connection comes from. The answer is used on the spot and isn't stored.

When you send us a message

The contact form asks for your name, email address and message. We use them to read and reply to you, and for nothing else.

  • The form sends your message to a small mail service we run on Render. It passes the message to Resend, our email delivery provider, which delivers it to our inbox.
  • The mail service doesn't store or log what you write. To limit spam it counts messages per IP address in memory for up to an hour, then forgets the address.
  • We keep your message in our inbox for as long as we need it to help you and to keep a record of support requests, then delete it. Resend keeps a delivery log for a limited period under its own policy.

When you buy the folder

You pay on a checkout page run by Polar Software, Inc., our online reseller and merchant of record, not on this website. Polar collects your payment details, processes the payment, handles tax and sends your receipt and download link. We never see your card number. We receive your name, email address, country and order details so we can deliver your PDF, answer questions, handle refunds and keep sales records.

Polar's privacy policy covers what it collects. Its checkout page may set cookies. This website does not.

Who we share information with

Only the service providers named above, and only so they can do their job: Render (hosting), Resend (email delivery) and Polar (checkout, payments and file delivery). These providers may process information in countries other than yours. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and ask us to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law. Use the contact page and we'll answer by email. We may need to keep some order records where the law requires it.

Children

The Boxing Folder is written for adults and isn't aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

The website and the mail service use HTTPS, and the mail service accepts messages only from this website. No system is perfectly secure, which is one reason we keep what we collect to a minimum.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Questions

Write to us through the contact page.
